# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy and take courses.

## Language

### Buying

**Purchase**:
A learner's payment for a course. It is permanent: giving money back attaches to the Purchase and never removes it.

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to have the money for a Purchase given back. It is Requested, then either Approved or Declined.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. It ends the learner's access to the course, and the Purchase remains.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days from a Purchase during which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can have a Refund Request raised on it: inside the Refund Window, not too much of the course watched or completed, and not a claimed team seat.

**Chargeback**:
A dispute a buyer raises with their bank to force a payment back. It is not a Refund and must never be used to mean one.
